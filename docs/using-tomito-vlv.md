# Use Tomito vlv

## Timer

Press **Start** to begin a session. Press **Pause** to freeze it, then **Resume** to continue. The overflow menu provides Stop, Restart, and Skip.

When a session ends, Tomito vlv moves to a short break or long break. A long break occurs after the configured number of completed sessions.

## Menu bar

Select the timer icon in the macOS menu bar to start, pause, restart, skip, open Preferences, or quit. The menu bar stays available when the main window is hidden.

## Preferences

### General

Set session, short-break, and long-break durations. Choose the long-break cycle and automatic starts. Disable breaks when you want session-only timing.

### Appearance

Choose System, Light, or Dark mode. Choose Tomato or Forest accent. Choose `🇺🇸 English` or `🇲🇽 Español`; the app starts in English.

### Advanced

Enable **Keep Mac awake while timer is active** to prevent display sleep during an active timer. You can also pause on Mac sleep, resume on wake, hide the window when the timer starts, show it when a phase ends, or keep it in front.
